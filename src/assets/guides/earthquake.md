# Earthquake Safety Guide

**WARNING: Do not run outside during shaking! Stay where you are until the shaking stops.**

1. **Drop, Cover, and Hold On**
   - **Drop** to your hands and knees.
   - **Cover** your head and neck with your arms. If a sturdy table or desk is nearby, crawl underneath it for shelter.
   - **Hold On** to your shelter (or to your head and neck) until the shaking stops.

2. **If you are inside:**
   - Stay inside.
   - Move away from windows, glass, and exterior doors.
   - Do not use elevators.

3. **If you are outside:**
   - Move away from buildings, streetlights, and utility wires.
   - Once in the open, stay there until the shaking stops.

4. **If you are in a vehicle:**
   - Stop as quickly as safety permits and stay in the vehicle.
   - Avoid stopping near or under buildings, trees, overpasses, and utility wires.

5. **After the shaking stops:**
   - Check yourself and others for injuries.
   - Expect aftershocks.
   - Listen to a battery-operated radio or TV for the latest emergency information (available in OneHelp SOS broadcast).
