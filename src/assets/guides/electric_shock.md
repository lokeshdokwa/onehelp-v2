# Electric Shock Guide

**WARNING: Do NOT touch the person if they are still in contact with the electrical source. You will be shocked too.**

1. **Turn Off the Power**
   - The first and most critical step is to separate the person from the source of the current.
   - Turn off the main circuit breaker or unplug the appliance if it's safe to do so.

2. **Separate the Person from the Source (if power cannot be turned off)**
   - Stand on something dry and non-conducting (like a rubber mat or a stack of newspapers).
   - Use a dry, non-conducting object (like a wooden broom handle, a plastic chair, or a heavy rubber glove) to push the person away from the electrical source or to push the source away from the person.

3. **Call for Medical Help**
   - Call 108 or 112 immediately, even if the person seems fine. Internal burns or heart rhythm disruptions can be life-threatening and not immediately obvious.

4. **Check for Breathing and Pulse**
   - Once the person is clear of the electrical source, check their responsiveness.
   - If they are not breathing or have no pulse, begin CPR immediately.

5. **Provide First Aid**
   - If the person is breathing and has a pulse, check for burns.
   - Do not apply ice, butter, or ointments to burns. Cover them loosely with a sterile gauze bandage.
   - Keep the person warm to prevent shock while waiting for the ambulance.
