# Poisoning Response Guide

**WARNING: Do NOT induce vomiting unless instructed to do so by a medical professional or poison control center.**

1. **Ensure Safety**
   - If the poison is inhaled, get the person to fresh air immediately.
   - If the poison is on the skin, take off any clothing the poison touched. Rinse skin with running water for 15 to 20 minutes.
   - If the poison is in the eyes, rinse eyes with running water for 15 to 20 minutes.

2. **Call for Help**
   - Call the National Poison Information Centre (AIIMS) at 1800-116-117 or 011-26593677, or call 108 for an ambulance.
   - Have the poison container (pill bottle, chemical bottle) with you when you call so you can describe exactly what was taken.

3. **Provide Information**
   - Be ready to provide the person's age, weight, and symptoms.
   - Tell the operator what the person took, how much, and when.

4. **Do NOT Give Home Remedies**
   - Do not give the person anything to eat or drink unless told to do so by Poison Control.
   - Do not use syrup of ipecac to induce vomiting.

5. **If Unconscious or Not Breathing**
   - Call 108 immediately.
   - Begin CPR if you are trained and the person is not breathing. If the poisoning is from a chemical or pill, avoid mouth-to-mouth if the poison is on their lips; use chest compressions only or a mask.
