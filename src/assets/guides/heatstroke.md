# Heatstroke Treatment Guide

**WARNING: Heatstroke is a medical emergency. Delaying treatment can be fatal. Call 108 immediately.**

1. **Recognize the Signs**
   - High body temperature (103°F or higher).
   - Hot, red, dry, or damp skin.
   - Fast, strong pulse.
   - Headache, dizziness, nausea, confusion.
   - Losing consciousness (passing out).

2. **Move to a Cooler Place**
   - Move the person to a shaded, cool area or an air-conditioned room.

3. **Lower Body Temperature**
   - Help lower the person's body temperature with cool cloths or a cool bath.
   - Place cold, wet cloths or ice packs on the neck, armpits, and groin.
   - Fan the person continuously.

4. **Do NOT Give Fluids (if unconscious)**
   - Do not give the person anything to drink if they are vomiting or unconscious.
   - If they are awake and alert, give them sips of cool water (not ice cold) or a sports drink. Do not give them alcohol or caffeinated beverages.

5. **Wait for Medical Help**
   - Stay with the person until emergency medical personnel arrive. Continue cooling efforts.
