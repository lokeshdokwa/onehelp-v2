# Flood Escape Guide

**WARNING: Do not walk, swim, or drive through flood waters. Turn Around, Don't Drown!**

1. **Evacuate Immediately**
   - If told to evacuate, do so immediately. Never ignore evacuation orders.
   - Move to higher ground or a higher floor.

2. **Avoid Floodwaters**
   - Just 6 inches of moving water can knock you down, and one foot of moving water can sweep your vehicle away.
   - Stay off bridges over fast-moving water.

3. **Prepare Your Home (if time permits)**
   - Turn off utilities at the main switches or valves if instructed to do so.
   - Disconnect electrical appliances. Do not touch electrical equipment if you are wet or standing in water.

4. **During the Flood**
   - Do not drive into flooded areas. If floodwaters rise around your car, abandon the car and move to higher ground if you can do so safely.
   - Avoid wading in floodwater, which can be contaminated and contain dangerous debris. Underground or downed power lines can also electrically charge the water.

5. **After the Flood**
   - Listen for news reports to learn whether the community's water supply is safe to drink.
   - Avoid floodwaters; water may be contaminated by oil, gasoline, or raw sewage.
