# Snake Bite First Aid Guide

**WARNING: Do NOT attempt to suck out the venom, cut the wound, or apply a tourniquet. These methods are dangerous and ineffective.**

1. **Stay Calm and Call for Help**
   - Keep the victim calm and still. Movement can cause the venom to spread faster.
   - Call 108 or 112 immediately. Note the time of the bite.

2. **Immobilize the Area**
   - Remove any rings, watches, or tight clothing near the bite, as swelling will occur rapidly.
   - Keep the bitten area still and positioned at or slightly below the level of the heart.

3. **Clean the Wound**
   - Gently wash the bite with soap and water if available.
   - Cover the bite with a clean, dry dressing.

4. **Do NOT Do the Following:**
   - Do NOT apply a tourniquet.
   - Do NOT cut the bite wound.
   - Do NOT try to suck out the venom.
   - Do NOT apply ice or immerse the wound in water.
   - Do NOT give the person alcohol or caffeinated drinks.
   - Do NOT try to catch the snake. Try to remember its color and shape to tell medical staff.

5. **Transport to a Hospital**
   - Get the victim to a hospital immediately. Anti-venom is the only effective treatment for venomous snake bites.
