# Choking Response Guide (Heimlich Maneuver)

**WARNING: Only perform the Heimlich Maneuver if the person cannot breathe, cough, or speak. If they can cough forcefully, encourage them to keep coughing.**

1. **Assess the Situation**
   - Ask: "Are you choking?"
   - If the person nods or cannot answer, act immediately.

2. **Give 5 Back Blows**
   - Stand to the side and just behind a choking adult.
   - Place one arm across the person's chest for support. Bend the person over at the waist so that the upper body is parallel with the ground.
   - Deliver five separate back blows between the person's shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.

3. **Give 5 Abdominal Thrusts (Heimlich Maneuver)**
   - Stand behind the person.
   - Place your arms around their waist and bend them forward.
   - Clench a fist and place it right above their belly button.
   - Grab the fist with your other hand.
   - Make a quick, upward thrust into the abdomen as if trying to lift the person up.

4. **Alternate Blows and Thrusts**
   - Perform 5 back blows followed by 5 abdominal thrusts until the blockage is dislodged.

5. **If the Person Becomes Unconscious**
   - Lower them to the floor.
   - Call emergency medical help (108 / 112).
   - Begin CPR, starting with chest compressions. Look in the mouth for the object before giving rescue breaths.
