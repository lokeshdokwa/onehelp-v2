# Road Accident Response Guide

**WARNING: Do not move severely injured victims unless they are in immediate danger of fire or explosion. Moving them can worsen spinal injuries.**

1. **Secure the Scene**
   - Stop your vehicle safely and turn on hazard lights.
   - Use warning triangles or flares to alert other drivers.
   - Turn off the engine of the crashed vehicle if possible.

2. **Call for Help**
   - Call 108 for medical emergency or 112 for the police.
   - Use the OneHelp SOS feature to automatically dispatch your coordinates.

3. **Check for Injuries**
   - Assess the number of injured people and the severity of their injuries.
   - Check for breathing and severe bleeding.

4. **Provide First Aid (if trained)**
   - **Stop bleeding:** Apply direct pressure to wounds using a clean cloth or bandage.
   - **Do not move victims:** Unless there is an immediate threat to life (like a fire), keep the injured person still to prevent spinal cord damage.
   - **Talk to the conscious victims:** Keep them calm and reassure them that help is on the way.

5. **Do Not Give Food or Drink**
   - Injured victims may need surgery, and having food or water in their stomach can cause complications.
